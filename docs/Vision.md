# Vision

## What the app is

A digital table for *Homeworld: Revelations*. It **generates a campaign**, **runs it** with an AI Game Master (or helps a human one), and handles everything the tabletop does by hand: dice, Momentum and Threat, character sheets, ship stations, fleet battles, and the long memory of a campaign. It grew out of campaigns played with Claude as GM.

## Who it's for

- Groups of **1–6 players** (the corebook's recommended range tops out at 6), online or around a TV.
- Solo players who want an AI GM.
- Human GMs who want a smart GM screen and prep assistant.

## Pillars

1. **Rules-true.** The 2d20 engine is implemented faithfully, and house rules are explicit toggles.
2. **It feels like Homeworld.** Fleet-scale battles that zoom into bridge-crew drama, sparse industrial ships, loss and hope, the journey home.
3. **The AI GM is honest.** Mechanics go through tools, every Threat spend has a visible cause, and the human can always override.
4. **A full table stays fun.** Turn structure, strike forces, fleet mode and spotlight balancing keep six players engaged.
5. **Never lose a campaign.** Event-sourced saves, undo, recaps, export.
