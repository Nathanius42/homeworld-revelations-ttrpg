# Suggested Features

Useful features beyond the core loop, grouped and roughly prioritised (**P1** = soon after the MVP, **P2** = later, **P3** = nice to have).

## At the table

- **P1 Session-zero toolkit:** campaign pitch, tone dials, lines and veils, an X-card or pause button during play, and an anonymous "fade to black" request.
- **P1 Scene recap and "previously on…":** AI-written from the event log, read out at the start of each session.
- **P1 Rules lookup:** a searchable quick reference in our own words, with page refs, available in-scene ("what does Vicious do?").
- **P1 Initiative and turn notifications:** "your turn" pings on phones; a gentle nudge timer for slow turns.
- **P2 Voice mode:** the AI GM narrates through TTS, and players can speak their actions (STT).
- **P2 Ambient soundscapes and music** by scene type (bridge, derelict, battle), with the volume under GM control.
- **P2 Handouts:** the GM pushes images, intercepted transmissions, star charts and logs to players, with reveal control.
- **P2 Player journals:** private notes, plus a shared crew log.
- **P3 Physical dice capture:** players type in or photograph rolls from the real dice set.

## Art

- **P1 Art studio:** generate Homeworld-style ship renders, portraits, location panoramas and handouts on demand from the GM Board, using the local ComfyUI pipeline with house style presets, and cache them per campaign.
- **P2 Scene art by the AI GM:** the AI GM requests a panorama for each new location, and the Board shows it in the player-face header.

## GM and AI GM

- **P1 GM override and audit:** every AI decision (difficulty, Threat spend, NPC action) has a "why" and a one-click override.
- **P1 Prep assistant:** "build me a 2-hour mission for 6 players set in the Karos Graveyard". It returns an editable mission skeleton.
- **P1 NPC quick-gen and name tables**, with identifying details for crowds.
- **P2 Improv buttons:** random complication, random space phenomenon, random derelict contents, random Turanic demand.
- **P2 Campaign wiki:** automatic pages for NPCs, locations and factions as they're met (a player-facing codex that unlocks as discovered).
- **P2 Relationship map:** a graph of PCs, NPCs, Kiithid and factions, with attitudes that change in play.
- **P3 Faction clocks:** off-screen agendas (Taiidan pursuit, Kadeshi zeal) advancing between sessions.

## Fleet and strategy (very Homeworld)

- **P1 Tactical zone map:** 3D-ish zone bubbles, ship tokens, ranges and sensor range. Shared with everyone.
- **P2 Fleet layer:** a fleet roster, a resource (RU) economy with collectors and controllers, construction queues on the Mothership, and research projects as extended tests. A light RTS layer between missions.
- **P2 Hyperspace jump sequence:** the cinematic transition between missions (a mission map of the Exodus route).
- **P3 Battle replay:** watch a fleet battle back from the event log.

## Progression and community

- **P1 Character and ship sharing:** export and import, and template libraries.
- **P2 Achievements and the fleet record:** commendations, kills, missions and the dead, kept in memoriam (fits the setting's tone of loss).
- **P2 Campaign templates:** share generated campaigns by seed.
- **P3 Homebrew editor:** custom talents, ships, weapons and NPCs through the data-pack DSL, with validation.

## Platform

- **P1 Responsive web app** (desktop, tablet, phone player view) and a **shared TV view** for in-person tables.
- **P1 Accessibility:** screen-reader dice readouts, colour-blind-safe palettes, reduced motion, text scaling.
- **P2 Discord integration:** a bot posts rolls and scene cards, and voice play happens there.
- **P2 Offline solo mode** with the AI GM and a local save.
- **P3 Localisation framework** (Australian English is the base locale).
